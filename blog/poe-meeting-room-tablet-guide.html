POE Meeting Room Tablets: The Complete
Guide to Smart Office Displays

📅 April 2025⏱ 9 min read✍ BESTAR TEK Product Team

Walk into any modern office building and you'll likely see tablets mounted outside conference rooms — showing who booked the room, when it's free, and a quick way to grab an available slot. These are meeting room booking displays, and the shift to Power-over-Ethernet (POE) as the standard power and data delivery method has fundamentally changed how they're deployed.

This guide explains how POE meeting room tablets work, what features matter for commercial deployments, how to integrate them with your room booking platform, and what to look for when selecting an OEM manufacturer.

Key takeaway: POE eliminates the need for a separate power cable at each meeting room door. One Ethernet cable carries both data and power — reducing installation cost by 40–60% compared to traditional powered displays and enabling clean, cable-free installations.

1. How POE Powers Meeting Room Tablets

Power over Ethernet (POE) is defined by the IEEE 802.3 standard family. For meeting room displays, the relevant standard is:

  • IEEE 802.3af (POE): Delivers up to 15.4W — sufficient for small (8"–10") displays with low-power processors
  • IEEE 802.3at (POE+): Delivers up to 30W — the standard for 10.1"–15.6" commercial meeting room tablets with full Android operation, LED strips, and NFC
  • IEEE 802.3bt (POE++): Delivers up to 90W — used in specialist high-brightness or large format displays

All BESTAR TEK D-Series meeting room tablets support IEEE 802.3at (POE+), which powers the display, Android OS, WiFi, Ethernet communication, LED status light, and optional peripherals through the single RJ45 cable.

2. The LED Status Ring: Why It Matters

One of the most important features of a commercial meeting room display — and one frequently overlooked in spec sheets — is the tri-color LED status indicator. This LED strip runs along the side bezels of the tablet and is visible from 10–15 meters down a corridor.

LED ColorStatus MeaningUse Case
🟢 GreenRoom availableWalk-up instant booking possible
🔴 RedRoom occupied / bookedDo not disturb — meeting in progress
🟠 Orange / YellowMeeting ending soon / check-in pendingBooking about to expire or needs confirmation

This simple traffic-light system reduces "hallway interruptions" in busy offices — staff can see room status before they even approach the door. All BESTAR TEK D-Series models include bilateral LED strips (both side bezels) for maximum visibility.

3. Integrating with Room Booking Platforms

A meeting room tablet is only as useful as the software it runs. Most enterprise customers need integration with one of these platforms:

  • Microsoft Exchange / Microsoft 365 — Calendar API integration, Teams Rooms compatibility
  • Google Workspace (Google Calendar) — Calendar resource booking via Google API
  • Dedicated room booking software — Robin, Condeco, ENVOY, EMS, NFS Rendezvous, AskCody
  • Custom internal booking systems — REST API or webhook integration

The Android platform on our D-Series tablets gives your software full control of the LED strip via our exposed GPIO/SDK, access to the NFC reader (for tap-to-book or badge check-in), and HDMI output to drive a secondary hallway display simultaneously.

5. Available Sizes: 10.1", 14", and 15.6"

The right size depends on your installation context and software UI:

  • 10.1" (D101T Series): The most common meeting room display size. Fits standard wall junction boxes, clear viewing from 2–3 meters. Ideal for small to medium meeting rooms (4–12 people).
  • 14" (D140T Series): Better for boardrooms or large meeting spaces where the display needs to be readable from 4–5 meters. Also popular for hotel conference facilities.
  • 15.6" (D156T Series): Suited for large conference centers, auditoriums, or multi-function spaces. Can simultaneously drive a secondary hallway display via HDMI output.

6. Installation Considerations

Clean installation is critical for enterprise environments. Here's the standard deployment approach for BESTAR TEK D-Series tablets:

  1. Run a single Cat6 Ethernet cable from the nearest POE switch to the meeting room door
  2. Install an 86mm flush-mount wall junction box (standard size, sold separately)
  3. Connect the D-Series tablet mounting bracket to the junction box
  4. Snap the tablet onto the bracket — the RJ45 connector engages simultaneously
  5. Power-on is automatic from the POE switch — no additional power connection needed
  6. Android boots, your room booking app auto-launches from the OEM-configured firmware

Total installation time per room with a pre-configured device: under 15 minutes.
