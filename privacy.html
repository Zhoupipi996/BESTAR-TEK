Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Editorial note: This is a standard draft privacy policy for a B2B manufacturing website — it is not a substitute for legal advice. Please have it reviewed by counsel familiar with the data-protection laws of your markets (e.g. GDPR for the EU, PIPL for China, CCPA for California) before publishing, and update the contact details and any specifics (analytics tools, form provider, cookie list) to match what you actually have running on the live site.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how BESTAR ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit bestartek.com or contact us through the site. Our registered office is 2101, Building A, Xinghe Century, No. 3069 Caitian Road, Gangxia Community, Futian Street, Futian District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as through our contact / RFQ form: your name, company, email address, phone number (if provided), product interest, target market, estimated volume, and any project details you share. We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or other sensitive identifiers through this site.

We may also collect standard technical information automatically when you browse the site — such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring site — typically via analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics, once connected) and server logs.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry, quotation request, or sample request.
  • To prepare and send certification, specification, or pricing documentation you've asked for.
  • To improve this website and understand which pages and products visitors find useful.
  • To comply with legal, accounting, and export-documentation obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your contact details for unrelated marketing unless you separately opt in.

4. Cookies & similar technologies

This site may use cookies or similar local storage to remember basic preferences and to measure traffic through analytics tools. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some site features may not function as intended if you do.

5. Sharing your information

We may share the information you submit with: (a) our internal sales and engineering team to process your inquiry; (b) our certified manufacturing partners, strictly where needed to quote or fulfill a specific order; (c) service providers who help us operate the site (such as a form-submission or email delivery provider) under confidentiality obligations; and (d) authorities where required by law (for example, export/customs documentation).

6. Data retention

We retain inquiry and order-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described above and to meet our legal, accounting, or export-recordkeeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. International transfers

As a China-based manufacturer serving international B2B partners, information you submit may be processed in China and transferred to other countries where we or our service providers operate, in each case with appropriate safeguards.

9. Children's privacy

This site is intended for business buyers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to sales@bestartek.com or through our contact form.
