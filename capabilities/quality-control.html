Structured quality control, on every order

Four inspection stages run on every order, whether it's a 500-unit ready-inventory purchase or a full ODM development project.

01

Incoming Material Inspection

Components and raw materials checked against spec before production begins.

02

In-Process Inspection

Line checks during assembly to catch defects before they compound.

03

Final Product Inspection

Completed units tested against full functional and cosmetic spec.

04

Pre-Shipment Random Sampling

Statistical sampling from the finished batch before it leaves the factory.
