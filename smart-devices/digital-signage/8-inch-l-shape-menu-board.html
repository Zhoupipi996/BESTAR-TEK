8-Inch L-Shape Digital Menu Board OEM Manufacturer

The 8-inch L-Shape Digital Menu Board is an interactive digital signage unit built for countertop retail and POS deployments — its L-shape stand keeps the display upright without a separate mounting bracket. Running Android 11 with 2GB+32GB or 4GB+32GB memory options, it's supplied to POS software vendors and retail-fixture brands as a white-label digital signage tablet manufacturer product. Light OEM branding (stand color, boot logo) or full ODM (different stand geometry) both available.
