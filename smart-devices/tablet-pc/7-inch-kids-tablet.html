7-Inch Kids Android Tablet PC — OEM Educational Tablet

The 7-inch Kids Tablet is BESTAR's entry-level OEM Android tablet PC purpose-built for early-childhood education programs. Preloaded with kid-friendly interactive educational apps and puzzle games, it's supplied to school-supply brands, edtech startups, and toy manufacturers who want a bulk Android tablet for business without building a device from zero. As a white-label Android tablet manufacturer, BESTAR can adapt the home-screen launcher, parental-control profile, and packaging to your brand under our Light OEM tier, or move to full ODM if you need a different form factor or screen size.
