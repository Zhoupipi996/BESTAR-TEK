15.6-Inch All-in-One Android Tablet PC for Education

BESTAR's largest standard OEM Android tablet PC, the 15.6-inch All-in-One model, runs Android 12 on an octa-core 2.0GHz processor with 6GB RAM / 128GB ROM, a front 5.0MP camera, and AI-recognition support — specified for student-facing all-in-one learning terminals and classroom kiosks. Its scale also makes it a natural crossover into digital-signage-style deployments; ask about our Interactive Digital Signage line if your use case is display-first rather than tablet-first.
