About BESTAR

Hardware for the people building smarter systems

BESTAR has manufactured Android tablet PCs and smart touchscreen terminals since 2018, providing a turnkey path from concept to shipment for software and appliance partners. Since 2026 the company has organized its work into two divisions — Smart Devices and Eco Appliances.

What we do

We provide a turnkey solution for touchscreen terminal hardware customization, manufacturing, and shipment to our global partners. With over 7 years in tablet PCs and smart touch display terminals, we help software partners fulfill their projects with stable products and seamless integration.

We support partners in Education & E-Learning, Smart Home, Healthcare, Fitness & Self-Service Kiosks, Retail, POS & Hospitality, Industrial Control, Logistics & Fleet Management, and Government & Public Service Terminals.
